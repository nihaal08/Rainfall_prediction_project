About RainCast AI

RainCast AI leverages machine learning to provide accurate rainfall predictions, helping farmers, researchers, and weather enthusiasts make informed decisions.

Our platform uses a Random Forest model trained on historical weather data to predict rainfall based on parameters like:

  • Pressure
  • Dewpoint
  • Humidity
  • Cloud Cover
  • Sunshine
  • Windspeed

Built with Flask, our application ensures a seamless user experience.
